1.5 Contribution

Ez nagyon fontos.

Példa:

The main contributions of this work are:

1. An adaptive elite swarm optimization framework for online robot kinematic calibration.

2. A diversity-driven adaptive swarm control mechanism using positional and velocity diversity metrics.

3. An elite-aging strategy for reducing swarm stagnation during online optimization.

4. A comprehensive comparison against classical PSO, adaptive PSO, DE, and GA methods.
